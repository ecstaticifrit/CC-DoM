# 15: Make the pilot offer measurable

**What to build:** Give the buyer a priced offer and a read-only shadow-pilot scorecard. Let them test whether Kavach saves enough work to justify the fee without claiming that a held payment is a saving.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] State Rs 2,000 per weekly run of up to 100 bills, including evidence and follow-up tracking for up to five exceptions. Bank payout fees are separate. Agree extra work in advance; do not price per alert.
- [ ] Distinguish Kavach's evidence gathering and follow-up from the customer's callback and separate beneficiary authorization. Describe a paid, read-only pilot; running the pilot or contacting a vendor is outside this ticket.
- [ ] Provide a scorecard comparing the same historical batch with and without Kavach. Include callback time in both methods. Record total handling minutes, false holds, and late clean payments.
- [ ] Separately record Kavach's API/model spend, conversion rate, sponsored credits, and staff time. Mark illustrative inputs as illustrative.
- [ ] Show the arithmetic: at Rs 250 per hour the fee requires eight staff-hours saved; at Rs 500 it requires four. Exclude the face value of held bills from savings.
