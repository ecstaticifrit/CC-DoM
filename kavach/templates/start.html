Quick start

  1. Choose Pay approved bills or Post bank lines.
  2. Upload a CSV file, or use a sample file below.
  3. Select any row to see every check, its source and what it proves.
  4. Sign the clean bills, then download the payment file and the receipt.

Sample files

  • Speed Post tariff bills (Pay approved bills): published INR prices, fictional bills and test-only accounts.
  • UK public spending (Post bank lines): two published DWP hosting payments in GBP, not a bank statement.
  • friday_bills.csv — 5 bills: 3 clean, 1 bank account change, 1 possible duplicate
  • brief_bank_lines.csv — 4 bank lines: Swiggy, AWS, a second AWS charge, and an unknown payee
  • friday_bank_statement.csv — test bank statement. Upload it on the run page after you sign the sample bills.

Columns the CSV needs

bill_id, carrier_id, carrier_name, amount, currency, account_number, ifsc, sender_email
