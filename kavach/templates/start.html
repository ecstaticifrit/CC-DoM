Quick start

  1. Choose a job and upload a CSV file. Then review any holds.
  2. Bills: sign the clean bills to make the payment file for your bank. Kavach never sends money.
  3. Bills: after payment, upload the bank statement on the same run to confirm what cleared.
  4. Bank lines: each supported line gets a legal payee, a ledger account and a posting ID.

Demo files

  • demo_bills.csv (Pay approved bills): 8 test bills that show every check: 3 clean, a changed bank account, a lookalike sender, a possible duplicate, a missing account number and an unknown carrier.
  • brief_bank_lines.csv (Post bank lines): 4 bank lines. Swiggy, AWS, a second identical AWS line and an unknown payee.

Columns the CSV needs

Pay approved bills:

bill_id, carrier_id, carrier_name, amount, currency, account_number, ifsc, sender_email

Post bank lines:

date, description, amount, currency, reference
